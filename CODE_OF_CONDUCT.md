# Code of Conduct

Zink contributors and maintainers commit to a welcoming, respectful project.
Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publication of another
person's private information are unacceptable in project spaces, including
issues, pull requests, and discussions.

If you experience or witness unacceptable conduct, contact the maintainer
privately through [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/deepanwadhwa/zink/security/advisories/new)
or another private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
Reports will be reviewed promptly and as confidentially as practical. The
maintainer may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation
to protect the community.

This policy applies to all project spaces and to public conduct that directly
affects the project community.
